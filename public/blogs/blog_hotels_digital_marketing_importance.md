## 4. Ethical Hotel Marketing, Pricing Transparency & Guest Privacy

Hospitality marketing requires strict adherence to advertising ethics, legal regulations, and guest privacy standards. Hotel marketers must focus communications on authentic property photos, verified amenities, transparent room pricing, and local destination highlights—**never on deceptive room photos, fake availability warnings, hidden utility fees, or unauthorized guest photo exposures**.

```
Ethical Hotel Marketing vs Misleading Claims:
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Misleading & Unethical Claims     | Ethical & Responsible Hospitality Marketing       |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Publishing edited/fake room photos| Showcasing authentic, high-resolution property media|
|  Hidden resort fees & surprises    | Displaying transparent, all-inclusive room rates   |
|  Posting guest photos w/o consent  | Protecting guest privacy & securing written consent|
|  Fabricating awards or star ratings| Displaying verified guest reviews & accreditations|
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4 Rules of Ethical Hotel Marketing
1. **Accurately Represent Room & Property Photos:** Only publish current photos of rooms, views, dining venues, and amenities that accurately reflect guest experiences.
2. **Protect Guest Privacy:** Never post guest photos, celebrity stay details, or private event videos without explicit, written, signed media consent forms.
3. **Maintain Pricing Transparency:** Clearly state check-in/check-out policies, cancellation terms, taxes, and included amenities upfront to prevent billing friction.
4. **Display Verified Property Accreditations:** Highlight genuine eco-certifications, heritage heritage ratings, and verified safety accreditations accurately.
