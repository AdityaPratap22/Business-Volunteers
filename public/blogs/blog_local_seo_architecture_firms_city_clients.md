### 4 Essential Google Business Profile Optimizations

## 3. Directory Consistency & Citation Building (NAP Alignment)

Search engines verify your studio's physical location by cross-referencing your NAP (Name, Address, Phone number) data across multiple external professional directories.

```
Multi-Directory NAP Citation Verification:
[ Website Contact Page Data ] ◄─── Equal Matching ───► [ Google Business Profile ]
                                          │
                                  Equal Matching
                                          │
                                          ▼
            [ Council of Architecture / IIA / Architectural Digest Listings ]
```

### Citation Management Best Practices
* **Enforce Strict Character-for-Character Uniformity:** If your address uses *"Plot 12, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai"*, ensure it does not appear as *"Plt #12, BKC Complex, Mumbai"* on external directories. Inconsistencies confuse search algorithms, lowering local map authority.
* **Claim Relevant Architectural Listings:** Audit and claim your firm profiles on major platforms like the Council of Architecture (COA) directory, Indian Institute of Architects (IIA), Houzz, Architectural Digest Directory, and local trade portals.
* **Audit & Remove Duplicate Records:** Duplicate listings dilute local search authority. Merge or delete outdated directory profiles immediately.
