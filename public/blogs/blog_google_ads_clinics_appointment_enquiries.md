## 2. Campaign Architecture: High-Intent Keywords vs. Negative Keywords

The primary reason many clinics waste ad spend on Google PPC is running broad, un-targeted campaigns that attract job seekers, student researchers, or DIY home-remedy searchers rather than paying patients.

```
Clinic Google Ads Keyword Architecture:
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. HIGH-INTENT COMMERCIAL KEYWORDS (Target Exact & Phrase Match)               |
|  "dermatologist in Gurgaon", "dental clinic near Cyber City"                     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                       │
                                       ▼
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  2. NEGATIVE KEYWORDS (Exclude Non-Converting Traffic)                           |
|  -jobs, -hiring, -salary, -home remedy, -free, -cheap, -wikipedia, -pdf          |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3 Keyword Selection Rules for Clinic PPC
1. **Target Exact & Phrase Match Terms:** Focus ad budget on exact-match (e.g., `[dermatologist in Gurgaon]`) and phrase-match (e.g., `"dental clinic near me"`) keywords to prevent irrelevant ad triggers.
2. **Eliminate Non-Buying Intent:** Maintain an extensive negative keyword list excluding terms like *jobs, hiring, salary, home remedy, free, cheap, wikipedia, pdf, salary, and medical student*.
3. **Target Specific Local Geographic Zones:** Limit ad delivery to major residential neighborhoods, affluent suburban sectors, or commercial business districts.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Google Ads for Clinics

### How can Google Ads help clinics generate more appointment enquiries?
Google Ads targets prospective patients at the exact moment they search Google for specialty medical doctors, skin clinics, or dental consultations. Running targeted Search Ads brings motivated patients to your landing pages, driving immediate appointment requests.

### How much should a clinic spend on Google Ads?
Ad budgets vary based on specialty competition (e.g., general consultation vs. high-margin laser skin treatments or dental implants). Because healthcare patients often require ongoing care management, investing in targeted PPC campaigns yields high Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).

### What is the difference between broad match and exact match keywords in clinic PPC?
Broad match triggers ads for loosely related queries (including job searches, student homework, and home remedies), wasting ad budget. Exact match (`[keyword]`) triggers ads only when searchers type the precise commercial healthcare keyword, ensuring high lead quality.

### Why is landing page site speed critical for Google Ads success?
Google uses landing page speed and user experience to calculate Quality Scores. A sub-2 second landing page improves Quality Scores, lowers Cost-Per-Click (CPC), and prevents anxious patients from bouncing off.

### Can Google Ads guarantee a specific lead volume or appointment revenue?
No legitimate agency can promise guaranteed patient numbers, appointments, or revenue because campaign performance depends on local market demand, seasonal factors, consultation pricing, and clinic customer service execution. Professional PPC optimization systematically maximizes your conversion probability.

### How fast should a clinic respond to an online appointment inquiry?
Clinics should deliver automated appointment confirmations within 60 seconds. Utilizing automated WhatsApp API messaging increases inquiry-to-consultation conversion rates by over 300% compared to delayed manual callbacks.

### How do conversational AI search engines process paid search ads?
AI search engines parse ad extensions, landing page Schema markup, and review sentiment across web directories to recommend local medical clinics for conversational prompts.
