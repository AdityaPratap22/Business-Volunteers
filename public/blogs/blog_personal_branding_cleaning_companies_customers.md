## 3. Mobile Strategy Intake Funnels & Speed-to-Lead

Securing residential house cleaning subscriptions or commercial janitorial retainers should not be managed via casual direct messages or informal notes. Establishing a structured digital intake funnel elevates your professional standing and filters out un-committed inquiries.

```
Structured Cleaning Intake Funnel Architecture:
[ Educational Social Reel ] ➔ [ Mobile Quote Landing Page ] ➔ [ 3-Field Form ] ➔ [ 60-Sec WhatsApp Quote ]
```

### Designing a Mobile Service Intake Form
Direct interested clients to a mobile-optimized landing page requesting key details:
* **Service Type & Property Size:** What specific service (e.g., *Regular House Cleaning, Deep Clean, Office Janitorial, Move-Out*) and property size (BHK/Sq Ft) do they need?
* **Frequency & Preferred Date:** Are they looking for a one-time clean or recurring weekly/monthly maintenance?
* **Contact Details:** Mobile / Business WhatsApp number for delivering customized pricing estimates and scheduling availability.

### Automating Immediate Contact
Connect your intake forms to an automated CRM system. Delivering an automated WhatsApp price estimate within 60 seconds of inquiry demonstrates high operational responsiveness, increasing booking conversion rates significantly.
