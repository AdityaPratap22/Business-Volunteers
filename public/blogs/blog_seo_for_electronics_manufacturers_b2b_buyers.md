### Implementing Essential Industrial Schema Code
Embed structured Schema markup into your website's header:
* **`Organization` (or `Manufacturer`) Schema:** Defines business name, official logo, verified facility address, operating hours, compliance credentials, and customer service phone numbers.
* **`Product` Schema:** Specifies exact component model numbers, technical parameters, operating specifications, and availability parameters.
* **`FAQPage` Schema:** Formats technical FAQs directly for Google Rich Snippets and conversational AI search engines.
