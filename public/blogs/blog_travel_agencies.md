1. **Destination Inspiration (35%):** Weekly Reels from destinations — hidden gems, food, culture, accommodation walkthroughs
2. **Customer Stories (25%):** With permission — traveller photos, video testimonials, "Our customer just returned from..." posts
3. **Travel Education (20%):** Visa process guides, travel tips, packing lists, currency guides — high-share content
4. **Package Showcases (15%):** Seasonal deals, limited-seat offers with urgent CTAs
5. **Behind the Scenes (5%):** Team working on itineraries, vendor meetings, office culture — builds trust

**Optimal posting schedule:**
- Instagram Reels: 4/week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun)
- Instagram Stories: Daily — polls, questions, destination reveals
- Facebook: 3/week with link posts to blog/packages
- WhatsApp Status: Daily with availability updates and deals

## Chapter 6: Online Reviews & Reputation

Reviews are the #1 trust factor for travel agency selection. Travellers are about to hand you ₹1–15 lakh for their vacation — they will read your reviews carefully.

**Review collection workflow:**
1. Send WhatsApp review request link 3 days after return
2. Follow up with email 7 days after return
3. Share 5-star reviews on Instagram Stories (screenshot with customer permission)
4. Address every negative review with empathy and a resolution offer

Target: **100+ Google reviews at 4.7+ stars** before scaling paid advertising.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How do I compete with MakeMyTrip and OTAs?**
Don't compete on price — compete on expertise and service. Specialise (honeymoon expert, visa specialist, luxury travel) and market that specialisation. Agencies offering curated, personalised experiences retain customers for life.

**Q2: Which is more effective — Instagram or Google Ads for travel agencies?**
Both serve different stages. Google Ads captures active searchers (highest intent, highest conversion). Instagram builds aspiration and brand recall — travellers who saw your Bali reel months ago will think of you when planning their trip.

**Q3: How important is WhatsApp for travel agency operations?**
Critical. Indian travellers expect WhatsApp communication for travel planning. Set up WhatsApp Business API, automate the initial response, and handle the conversation personally from there. Agencies with fast WhatsApp response convert 3× better than those relying on email.

**Q4: Should I specialise in one type of travel or offer everything?**
Specialists dominate digital marketing. A "Bali honeymoon specialist" or "US visa expert" can outrank and out-convert a generalist with far less budget. Specialisation commands premium pricing and builds stronger brand recognition.

**Q5: How many Google reviews do I need before running paid ads?**
Aim for 50+ Google reviews at 4.5+ stars before aggressively scaling paid ads. Reviews close the sale — ads bring traffic.

**Q6: How do I market my visa services effectively?**
Visa-related searches have extremely high intent and relatively low competition compared to package searches. Create dedicated visa landing pages for each country, run Google Ads for "[country] visa agent [city]," and clearly display success rates and processing times.

**Q7: What is a realistic ROI from digital marketing for a travel agency?**
At ₹20,000–₹35,000/month, a well-optimised travel agency can expect 12–25 bookings. At ₹30,000–₹1,50,000 average booking value, this delivers exceptional ROI — often 10:1 or better.
