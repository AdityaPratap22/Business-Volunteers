## Chapter 1: Local SEO & Google Business Profile (GBP)

When someone types "wedding planner in Mumbai" or "corporate event organizers near me," you want your agency to be at the top of the Google Maps pack. Local SEO is non-negotiable.

### Optimizing Your Google Business Profile
1. **Claim and Verify:** Ensure you own your GBP listing.
2. **Keyword-Rich Title & Description:** Use terms like "Event Planner," "Wedding Organizer," and your city name.
3. **High-Quality Photos:** Upload photos of your best setups, stages, lighting, and happy clients weekly.
4. **Services Menu:** List specific services (e.g., Destination Weddings, Corporate Offsites, Birthday Parties) with starting prices.
5. **Google Posts:** Post regular updates about recent events, tips for planning, or seasonal offers.

### Vendor Discovery SEO
Your website should also rank for long-tail keywords. Create dedicated pages for specific venues or themes you excel at, e.g., "Best Decorators for Taj Palace Delhi" or "Goa Destination Wedding Planners."

## Chapter 2: Google Ads / PPC Strategy

When a user searches for event planners on Google, they have high intent. They need someone *now*. Google Ads helps you capture this demand instantly.

### Campaign Types for Event Managers
- **Search Campaigns:** Target keywords with high commercial intent. 
- **Performance Max:** Use visual assets to reach users across YouTube, Display, and Search.
- **Retargeting:** Show ads to users who visited your website but didn't fill out the inquiry form.

### Keyword Strategy & CPC Estimates (India)

| Keyword | Search Intent | Est. CPC (₹) | Target Audience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| event management company near me | High | ₹40 - ₹80 | Individuals/Corporates |
| corporate event planners in [City] | High | ₹60 - ₹120 | HR Managers, Founders |
| destination wedding planners India | Very High | ₹80 - ₹150 | Engaged Couples, Parents |
| best birthday party organizers | Medium | ₹30 - ₹60 | Parents |

*Tip: Always use negative keywords like "jobs," "salary," or "courses" to avoid wasting budget on job seekers.*

## Chapter 3: Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

Event management is a visual industry. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) are your best tools for showcasing your portfolio and generating leads.

### Angles & Ad Strategies
1. **Instagram Event Portfolio Reels:** Create fast-paced, highly engaging Reels showing the "Before & After" of an empty hall transforming into a magical wedding venue. Run these as engagement or lead generation ads.
2. **Carousel Ads:** Show different aspects of a single event (e.g., Stage, Food setup, Artist performance, Happy Crowd).
3. **Targeting Options:**
   - *Weddings:* Target "Newly engaged," "Friends of newly engaged," or users interested in wedding publications.
   - *Corporate Events:* Target job titles like "HR Manager," "CEO," "Founder," or users interested in specific industries.

## Chapter 4: WhatsApp Marketing (Scripts + Automation)

WhatsApp is the most powerful tool for client coordination and lead nurturing in India. Use WhatsApp Business API for automation and broadcast messaging.

### 4 WhatsApp Scripts for Event Planners

**1. The New Lead Auto-Reply (Instant)**
> "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to [Your Company]! 🎉 We'd love to help make your upcoming event spectacular. Could you please share a few details: 1. Date of Event 2. Approximate Guest Count 3. Type of Event. Our lead planner will call you shortly!"

**2. The Portfolio Showcase (24 Hours Later)**
> "Hello [Name], while we prepare a custom proposal for your [Event Type], here’s a quick look at some similar events we recently organized: [Link to YouTube/Insta Reel]. Let me know if any specific theme catches your eye! ✨"

**3. The Client Coordination Update (For Active Clients)**
> "Hi [Name], quick update on your wedding prep! 🌸 The florist has confirmed the orchids, and the DJ has sent the updated playlist for your review. Check the attached PDF. Let’s jump on a quick 5-min call tomorrow at 11 AM to finalize?"

**4. The Post-Event Review Request**
> "Hi [Name], it was an absolute pleasure organizing your big day! 🎊 We hope everything was perfect. If you have a moment, we would be incredibly grateful if you could share your experience here: [Google Review Link]. It helps us a lot!"

## Chapter 5: Social Media & Content Strategy

Your social media isn't just a gallery; it's a trust-building engine. 

- **LinkedIn for Corporate:** Share case studies on how you managed a 1,000-person offsite, handled logistics, and ensured high engagement. 
- **Instagram for Weddings/Parties:** Focus on aesthetics, behind-the-scenes (BTS) chaos, and client testimonials.
- **Content Pillars:**
  - *Educational:* How to choose a venue, budget planning tips.
  - *Inspirational:* Trending decor themes, lighting ideas.
  - *Social Proof:* Client video testimonials, media mentions.

## Chapter 6: Online Reviews & Reputation Management

In the event business, you are only as good as your last event. Online reputation can make or break your agency.

- Automate review requests via WhatsApp or Email 2 days after the event.
- Always reply to reviews, both positive and negative. If someone leaves a negative review about food being cold, apologize professionally and explain the steps taken to fix the issue.
- Create video testimonials. A 30-second video of the bride or CEO praising your work is worth 100 written reviews.

## Chapter 7: KPI Dashboard & Measurement

Don't spend money blindly. Track these Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

1. **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** How much are you spending to get one inquiry? (Aim for ₹200 - ₹500 depending on the event scale).
2. **Lead to Conversion Ratio:** Out of 100 leads, how many book you? (Good benchmark is 5-10%).
3. **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** For every ₹1 spent on ads, how much revenue is generated?
4. **Organic Traffic Growth:** Are more people finding you via Google Search?

## Chapter 8: Website Optimization & CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization)

A beautiful wedding portfolio website is useless if it doesn't convert visitors into leads.

- **Mobile First:** 80%+ of your traffic will come from mobile phones. 
- **Clear CTA:** "Get a Free Quote" or "Book a Consultation" should be prominent on every page.
- **Trust Signals:** Display badges (e.g., "As featured in WedMeGood"), total events completed, and client logos.

## FAQ Section

**1. How much should a mid-sized event company spend on digital marketing?**
Typically, ₹30,000 to ₹60,000 per month is a good starting point for a mix of SEO and paid ads in India.

**2. Which platform is better for corporate events: Google Ads or Meta Ads?**
Google Ads and LinkedIn are best for targeting specific intents and professional demographics, respectively, for corporate events.

**3. Do I need a website, or is Instagram enough?**
While Instagram is great for discovery, a website builds credibility, allows for SEO, and provides a structured portfolio that Instagram lacks.

**4. How can I stand out in a crowded market like Delhi or Mumbai?**
Niche down (e.g., eco-friendly weddings, tech-focused corporate events), build a strong Google Review profile, and use high-quality video content.

**5. How long does local SEO take to show results?**
Generally, it takes 3 to 6 months to see consistent rankings for competitive keywords like "event management company."

**6. Can WhatsApp marketing actually generate new clients?**
Yes, primarily through referrals and re-engaging past clients (e.g., a corporate client who hired you last year might need you again).

**7. How do I track leads from different platforms?**
Using tools like Google Analytics, UTM parameters, and a dedicated CRM (or even a well-managed Google Sheet) linked to your ads.
