## 4. Responsible Healthcare Marketing & Content Safety Guidelines

Healthcare marketing must prioritize ethical communication, patient privacy, and factual accuracy. Diagnostic centers must focus marketing on service accessibility, test preparation education, and brand trust—**never on individual medical diagnosis or fear-based messaging**.

```
Ethical Healthcare Marketing vs Misleading Claims:
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Misleading & Unsafe Marketing     | Ethical & Responsible Healthcare Marketing        |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Diagnosing diseases from symptoms | Providing general test preparation guidelines      |
|  Promising guaranteed health outcomes| Highlighting lab accuracy, turnaround time & SLAs |
|  Fear-based disease warnings       | Promoting preventive health awareness & checkups  |
|  Un-verified medical accreditations| Displaying genuine, verified lab credentials      |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4 Rules of Responsible Healthcare Communication
1. **Never Provide Individual Medical Diagnosis:** Ensure educational content explains *what tests measure* without telling readers that specific symptoms definitely indicate a disease.
2. **Encourage Physician Consultation:** Advise patients to consult qualified doctors to interpret test results and manage health treatment plans.
3. **Display Genuine Accreditations Only:** Feature authentic lab certifications and registration numbers without fabricating credentials.
4. **Protect Patient Privacy (HIPAA / Local Privacy Mandates):** Never publish patient names, test reports, or personal details without explicit written consent.
