## 2. Campaign Architecture: High-Intent Keywords vs. Negative Keywords

The primary reason many cafe businesses waste ad spend on Google PPC is running broad, un-targeted campaigns that attract job seekers, suppliers, or free recipe searchers rather than dining guests.

```
Cafe Google Ads Keyword Architecture:
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. HIGH-INTENT COMMERCIAL KEYWORDS (Target Exact & Phrase Match)               |
|  "specialty coffee shop in Gurgaon", "breakfast cafe near Cyber City"           |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                       │
                                       ▼
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  2. NEGATIVE KEYWORDS (Exclude Non-Converting Traffic)                           |
|  -jobs, -recipe, -machine, -franchise cost, -wholesale beans, -supplier, -salary|
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3 Keyword Selection Rules for Cafe PPC
1. **Target Exact & Phrase Match Terms:** Focus ad budget on exact-match (e.g., `[coffee shop in Gurgaon]`) and phrase-match (e.g., `"best breakfast cafe"`) keywords to prevent irrelevant ad triggers.
2. **Eliminate Non-Buying Intent:** Maintain an extensive negative keyword list excluding terms like *jobs, recipe, machine, wholesale, equipment, supplier, franchise cost, and salary*.
3. **Hyper-Local Radius Targeting:** Limit ad delivery to a tight 2-to-5-kilometer radius around your physical venue to ensure searchers can realistically visit your cafe.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Google Ads for Cafes

### How can Google Ads help cafes attract more local customers?
Google Ads targets consumers at the exact moment they search Google for nearby coffee, food, or workspace venues. Running targeted local search ads brings motivated buyers to your website and Google Maps profile, driving immediate physical walk-ins.

### How much should a cafe spend on Google Ads?
Ad budgets vary based on local market competition and keyword target density. A modest daily budget focused on hyper-local exact-match keywords within a 2-to-5-kilometer radius yields high Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) due to the high repeat customer Lifetime Value (LTV) of cafe guests.

### What is the difference between broad match and exact match keywords in cafe PPC?
Broad match triggers ads for loosely related queries (including job searches and recipe recipes), wasting ad budget. Exact match (`[keyword]`) triggers ads only when searchers type the precise commercial keyword, ensuring high lead quality.

### Why is landing page site speed critical for Google Ads success?
Google uses landing page speed and user experience to calculate Quality Scores. A sub-2 second landing page improves Quality Scores, lowers Cost-Per-Click (CPC), and prevents hungry visitors from bouncing off.

### Can Google Ads guarantee a specific foot-traffic volume or revenue?
No legitimate agency can promise guaranteed customer volumes, foot-traffic numbers, or sales revenue because campaign performance depends on dynamic factors such as weather, local competition, ad budget, food quality, and service execution. Professional PPC optimization systematically maximizes your conversion probability.

### How fast should a cafe respond to private event or booking inquiries?
Cafes should deliver automated confirmation within 60 seconds. Utilizing automated WhatsApp API messaging increases table booking conversions by over 300% compared to delayed manual responses.

### How do conversational AI search engines process paid search ads?
AI search engines parse ad extensions, landing page Schema markup, and review sentiment across web directories to recommend local cafes for conversational prompts.
