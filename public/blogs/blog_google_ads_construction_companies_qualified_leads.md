## 2. Campaign Architecture: High-Intent Keywords vs. Negative Keywords

The primary reason many construction companies waste ad spend on Google PPC is running broad, un-targeted campaigns that attract job seekers, DIY home repair researchers, or free material price check searchers rather than paying project clients.

```
Construction Google Ads Keyword Architecture:
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. HIGH-INTENT COMMERCIAL KEYWORDS (Target Exact & Phrase Match)               |
|  "commercial civil contractor Gurgaon", "turnkey villa builder near Cyber City"  |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                       │
                                       ▼
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  2. NEGATIVE KEYWORDS (Exclude Non-Converting Traffic)                           |
|  -jobs, -hiring, -salary, -diy, -free notes, -material price per kg, -wikipedia |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3 Keyword Selection Rules for Construction PPC
1. **Target Exact & Phrase Match Terms:** Focus ad budget on exact-match (e.g., `[commercial construction company Gurgaon]`) and phrase-match (e.g., `"turnkey villa builder near me"`) keywords to prevent irrelevant ad triggers.
2. **Eliminate Non-Buying Intent:** Maintain an extensive negative keyword list excluding terms like *jobs, hiring, salary, diy, free notes, material price per kg, wikipedia, free, cheap, drawing pdf, and tutorial videos*.
3. **Target Specific Local Geographic Zones:** Limit ad delivery to major commercial hubs, affluent residential sectors, or industrial corridors.

### What is the difference between broad match and exact match keywords in contractor PPC?
Broad match triggers ads for loosely related queries (including job searches, DIY home tutorials, and raw material prices), wasting ad budget. Exact match (`[keyword]`) triggers ads only when searchers type the precise commercial service keyword, ensuring high lead quality.

Google uses landing page speed and user experience to calculate Quality Scores. A sub-2 second landing page improves Quality Scores, lowers Cost-Per-Click (CPC), and prevents anxious developers and homeowners from bouncing off.

No legitimate agency can promise guaranteed contract numbers, project values, or revenue because campaign performance depends on local market demand, seasonal factors, pricing, and sales consultation execution. Professional PPC optimization systematically maximizes your conversion probability.

### How fast should a construction firm respond to an online project inquiry?
