## 3. Mobile Strategy Intake Funnels & Speed-to-Lead

Securing specialty medical consultations or corporate health retainers should not be managed via casual direct messages or informal notes. Establishing a structured digital intake funnel elevates your professional standing and filters out un-committed inquiries.

```
Structured Clinic Intake Funnel Architecture:
[ Educational Health Reel ] ➔ [ Mobile Booking Landing Page ] ➔ [ 3-Field Form ] ➔ [ 60-Sec WhatsApp Booking ]
```

### Designing a Mobile Consultation Intake Form
Direct interested patients to a mobile-optimized landing page requesting key details:
* **Specialty & Consultation Reason:** What specific specialty (e.g., *Dermatology, Dental, Ortho, Pediatrics*) and health concern do they need addressed?
* **Doctor Preference & Target Date:** Are they looking for a specific practitioner or earliest available opening?
* **Contact Details:** Mobile / Business WhatsApp number for delivering customized appointment options and pre-consultation guidelines.

### Automating Immediate Contact
Connect your intake forms to an automated CRM system. Delivering an automated WhatsApp appointment confirmation within 60 seconds of inquiry demonstrates high operational responsiveness, increasing booking conversion rates significantly.
