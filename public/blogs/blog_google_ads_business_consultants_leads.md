## 2. Campaign Architecture: High-Intent Keywords vs. Negative Keywords

The primary reason many consulting firms waste ad spend on Google PPC is running broad, un-targeted campaigns that attract job seekers, students, or free advice seekers rather than corporate decision-makers.

```
Consulting Google Ads Keyword Architecture:
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. HIGH-INTENT COMMERCIAL KEYWORDS (Target Exact & Phrase Match)               |
|  "management consulting firm in Gurgaon", "B2B SaaS growth consultant"          |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                       │
                                       ▼
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  2. NEGATIVE KEYWORDS (Exclude Non-Converting Traffic)                           |
|  -jobs, -salary, -internship, -free, -PDF, -courses, -careers, -template        |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3 Keyword Selection Rules for Consulting PPC
1. **Target Exact & Phrase Match Terms:** Focus ad budget on exact-match (e.g., `[strategy consultant in Gurgaon]`) and phrase-match (e.g., `"business consulting services"`) keywords to prevent irrelevant ad triggers.
2. **Eliminate Non-Buying Intent:** Maintain an extensive negative keyword list excluding terms like *jobs, salary, free, PDF download, courses, resume, templates, and entry-level*.
3. **Incorporate Location Intent:** Target specific high-density business districts (e.g., Gurgaon, Cyber City, Delhi NCR, BKC Mumbai) where corporate headquarters are located.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Google Ads for Business Consultants

### How can Google Ads help business consultants generate qualified B2B leads?
Google Ads targets corporate executives at the exact moment they actively search Google for specialized advisory solutions. Running high-intent search campaigns brings motivated buyers to your website, generating immediate discovery call requests.

### How much should a consulting firm spend on Google Ads?
Ad budgets vary based on geographic market competition and keyword target density. Investing in a targeted campaign with sufficient budget to capture high-intent exact-match keywords yields high Return on Investment (ROI) due to the high revenue value of consulting retainers.

### What is the difference between broad match and exact match keywords in consulting PPC?
Broad match triggers ads for loosely related queries (including job searches and free advice), wasting ad budget. Exact match (`[keyword]`) triggers ads only when searchers type the precise commercial keyword, ensuring high lead quality.

### Why is landing page site speed critical for Google Ads success?
Google uses landing page speed and user experience to calculate Quality Scores. A sub-2 second landing page improves Quality Scores, lowers Cost-Per-Click (CPC), and prevents mobile visitors from bouncing off.

### Can Google Ads guarantee a specific number of consulting leads or clients?
No legitimate agency can promise guaranteed lead volumes, CPLs, or signed contracts because campaign performance depends on dynamic factors such as market competition, ad budget, offer quality, and sales call execution. Professional PPC optimization systematically maximizes your conversion probability.

### How fast should a consulting firm respond to a PPC lead?
Consulting firms should deliver automated confirmation within 60 seconds. Utilizing automated WhatsApp API messaging increases strategy call booking rates by over 300% compared to delayed manual callbacks.

### How do conversational AI search engines process paid search ads?
AI search engines parse ad extensions, landing page Schema markup, and review sentiment across web directories to recommend consulting firms for conversational prompts.
