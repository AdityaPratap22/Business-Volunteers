## 2. Campaign Architecture: High-Intent Keywords vs. Negative Keywords

The primary reason many cleaning companies waste ad spend on Google PPC is running broad, un-targeted campaigns that attract job seekers, DIY cleaning searchers, or low-budget window shoppers rather than paying clients.

```
Cleaning Google Ads Keyword Architecture:
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. HIGH-INTENT COMMERCIAL KEYWORDS (Target Exact & Phrase Match)               |
|  "house cleaning services in Gurgaon", "commercial office cleaning near me"     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                       │
                                       ▼
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  2. NEGATIVE KEYWORDS (Exclude Non-Converting Traffic)                           |
|  -jobs, -hiring, -salary, -diy, -products, -supplies, -free, -cheap, -training   |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3 Keyword Selection Rules for Cleaning PPC
1. **Target Exact & Phrase Match Terms:** Focus ad budget on exact-match (e.g., `[house cleaning services in Gurgaon]`) and phrase-match (e.g., `"office cleaning company"`) keywords to prevent irrelevant ad triggers.
2. **Eliminate Non-Buying Intent:** Maintain an extensive negative keyword list excluding terms like *jobs, hiring, salary, diy, products, supplies, free, cheap, training, equipment, and home hacks*.
3. **Target Specific Local Geographic Zones:** Limit ad delivery to major residential neighborhoods, affluent suburban sectors, or commercial business districts.

### What is the difference between broad match and exact match keywords in cleaning PPC?
Broad match triggers ads for loosely related queries (including job searches, DIY hacks, and cleaning supplies), wasting ad budget. Exact match (`[keyword]`) triggers ads only when searchers type the precise commercial service keyword, ensuring high lead quality.

### Why is landing page site speed critical for Google Ads success?
Google uses landing page speed and user experience to calculate Quality Scores. A sub-2 second landing page improves Quality Scores, lowers Cost-Per-Click (CPC), and prevents busy homeowners from bouncing off.

No legitimate agency can promise guaranteed lead numbers, bookings, or revenue because campaign performance depends on local market demand, seasonal factors, pricing competitiveness, and customer service execution. Professional PPC optimization systematically maximizes your conversion probability.

### How fast should a cleaning company respond to an online quote inquiry?
