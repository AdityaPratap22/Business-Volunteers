## Frequently Asked Questions About Local Business Paid Ads

### What are local business paid ads, and how do they work?
Local business paid ads are geo-targeted marketing campaigns running across Google Search, Google Maps, Facebook, and Instagram that display promotional offers exclusively to users located within a specific geographic service radius (e.g., 3km to 15km).

### How do Google Search Ads capture "near me" local intent?
Google Search Ads target users actively typing location-based queries (such as *"dentist near me"* or *"plumber in Gurgaon"*). By using location extensions and radius targeting, ads appear at the top of local search results.

### What is the difference between Google Search Ads and Meta Social Ads for local businesses?
Google Search Ads capture immediate, active demand from users seeking instant local solutions. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) generate awareness and demand by showing visual videos and special offers to nearby residents as they browse social feeds.

### Why are call-only ad campaigns effective for local services?
Call-only campaigns display a prominent phone button on mobile search ads, allowing users to call your business directly with a single tap without needing to navigate through a landing page.

### Why is 60-second speed-to-lead critical for local service inquiries?
Local customers evaluating immediate service options expect instant communication. Delivering an automated WhatsApp rate card or booking link within 60 seconds increases customer confirmation rates by over 300%.

### Can small local businesses compete against large national brands using paid ads?
Yes. By utilizing hyper-local radius targeting (e.g., targeting a 5km radius around your store), small local businesses concentrate their ad budget exclusively on nearby residents, achieving higher conversion rates than broad national campaigns.

### Can an agency guarantee specific walk-in traffic, phone calls, or ROAS?
No. Local ad results depend on physical store location, pricing competitiveness, service quality, customer reviews, landing page speed, and front-desk sales follow-up. Ethical agencies optimize campaigns based on empirical data rather than making false guarantees.
