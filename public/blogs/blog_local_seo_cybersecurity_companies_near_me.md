### 5 Critical Rules of GBP Optimization
1. **Maintain Exact NAP Consistency:** Ensure your **N**ame, **A**ddress, and **P**hone number match identically across your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company page, and trade directories.
2. **Select the Correct Primary Category:** Set your primary category to *"Computer Security Service"* or *"Information Technology Consultant"*. Add secondary categories for specialized services.
3. **Publish Detailed Service Items:** Add explicit service entries with transparent scope overviews for Managed SOC, Vulnerability Assessments, Cloud Security Audits, and ISO Compliance.
4. **Upload High-Quality Office & Team Photos:** Feature professional photos of your corporate office, security operations center (SOC) facility, and executive leadership team to establish real-world credibility.
5. **Post Weekly Business Updates:** Share regular updates highlighting recent case studies, technical whitepapers, security checklists, and company news directly on your GBP feed.

## 4. Building Authentic Local B2B Reviews & Citation Consistency

Local map algorithms rely heavily on third-party verification signals. Inconsistent directory citations or a lack of client reviews will depress your local rankings.

```
The Local Authority Triad:
[ Exact NAP Directory Citations ] ──► [ Google Business Profile 3-Pack ] ◄── [ Authentic Client Reviews ]
```

### 3 Rules for Ethical Local Authority Building
1. **Never Buy Fake Reviews or Addresses:** Google's anti-spam algorithms actively penalize businesses utilizing virtual office address tricks, fake reviews, or P.O. Boxes. Always use genuine, verifiable business details.
2. **Implement a Structured Review Request Workflow:** Following successful security audits, contract renewals, or project handovers, send a personalized request link inviting client IT leaders to post honest reviews on Google.
3. **Respond Professionally to All Reviews:** Acknowledge client feedback promptly with professional, courteous responses that reinforce your commitment to engineering excellence.
