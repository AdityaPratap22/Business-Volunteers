## 4. Healthcare Advertising Ethics, Patient Privacy & Compliance

Healthcare marketing requires strict adherence to medical advertising ethics, legal regulations, and patient privacy standards. Hospital marketers must focus communications on authentic facility capabilities, verified doctor credentials, transparent pricing structures, and patient education—**never on exaggerated medical claims, guaranteed cure promises, unverified drug outcomes, or unauthorized patient health data exposure**.

```
Ethical Healthcare Marketing vs Misleading Claims:
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Misleading & Unethical Claims     | Ethical & Responsible Healthcare Marketing        |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Promising 100% guaranteed cures   | Educating on treatment protocols & success rates  |
|  Using scare tactics or false panic| Providing clear, reassuring health information    |
|  Publishing patient photos w/o consent| Protecting patient privacy & securing written consent|
|  Fabricating doctor degrees/awards | Displaying verified medical credentials & tenure  |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4 Rules of Ethical Hospital Marketing
1. **Accurately Represent Doctor Credentials:** Only advertise specializations, degrees, and board certifications that have been verified by accredited medical councils.
2. **Protect Patient Privacy (HIPAA / Local Privacy Laws):** Never post patient photos, medical histories, or video testimonials without explicit, written, signed consent forms.
3. **Avoid Guaranteed Treatment Outcomes:** Medical science involves individual variability; never promise 100% guaranteed cures, painless surgeries, or instant recoveries.
4. **Display Upfront Facility & Insurance Information:** Provide clear details regarding accredited facilities, accepted insurance/TPA partners, and consultation fee structures.
