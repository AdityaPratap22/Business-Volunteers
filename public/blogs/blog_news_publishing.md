## Chapter 1: Google News SEO & Publisher Center

### Getting Into Google News

Google News is a separate discovery system from regular Google Search. Eligibility requirements:
- News-focused content (not primarily SEO content farms)
- Clear authorship and publication dates
- Original reporting (not just aggregation)
- Mobile-optimized website

**Google Publisher Center setup:**
1. Submit your publication at publishercenter.google.com
2. Verify site ownership via Google Search Console
3. Create a news sitemap (news-specific XML sitemap)
4. Implement structured data (Article schema with datePublished, author, headline)

### Technical SEO for News Websites

| Technical Factor | Requirement | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Page speed | Under 2.5 seconds (LCP) | 🔴 Critical |
| AMP pages | Strongly recommended for news | 🔴 High |
| Mobile responsiveness | Required | 🔴 Critical |
| HTTPS | Required | 🔴 Critical |
| Article schema | Must have datePublished, author, headline | 🔴 High |
| News sitemap | Submit to Google Search Console daily | 🟡 High |
| Core Web Vitals | Pass all three (LCP, FID, CLS) | 🟡 High |

### AMP (Accelerated Mobile Pages)

AMP pages load 3–4× faster on mobile and get preferential treatment in Google News carousels and Top Stories. For news portals, AMP is not optional in 2026 — it's essential.

**What AMP gives you:**
- Google News carousel placement (massive traffic)
- Faster mobile loading → lower bounce rate → better SEO
- Google AMP Story format for visual news storytelling

## Chapter 6: Subscription & Paywall Strategy

India's digital subscription economy is maturing. The Hindu, Indian Express, and The Ken have proven that Indian readers will pay for quality journalism. The opportunity for regional and niche publishers is enormous.

**Freemium paywall model** (recommended):
- 80% of content free — builds audience and SEO
- 20% premium (exclusive investigations, data journalism, expert analysis, ad-free experience)
- Price: ₹99–₹299/month

**Subscription growth tactics:**
- Email capture before paywall (build the lead before the conversion)
- Trial offers: "First month ₹1" — high volume acquisition
- Annual subscription at 20% discount to improve LTV
- WhatsApp subscriber-only groups for premium subscribers (creates community lock-in)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How do I recover from a Google algorithm traffic drop?**
Diversify immediately — build WhatsApp channel, YouTube, and email newsletter. Audit your site for thin content, duplicate pages, and site speed issues. Focus on original reporting and unique data journalism that cannot be replicated by AI summaries.

**Q2: Should I implement a paywall?**
Yes, if you have consistent quality content and 1,00,000+ monthly readers. Start with a freemium model (most content free, exclusive content paid). The Scroll and The Wire have shown the model works in India.

**Q3: What is the best way to generate revenue beyond display ads?**
Branded content and newsletter sponsorships offer 10–100× higher revenue per impression than display ads. Build your branded content sales capability — create a media kit with audience demographics, reach, and engagement metrics.

**Q4: How important is video for news portals?**
Critical. YouTube news channels can generate AdSense revenue, attract national sponsorships, and build audiences independent of Google News algorithm. Even a simple smartphone-shot news analysis video outperforms text articles in distribution.

**Q5: How do I build a loyal paying subscriber base?**
Trust, exclusivity, and community. Offer exclusive investigations, expert analysis, and direct access to journalists. Create a subscriber WhatsApp group or Discord community. Make subscribers feel like insiders, not just paying customers.

**Q6: What is WhatsApp Channel and should I use it?**
WhatsApp Channels are a broadcast tool allowing any publisher to send news updates to subscribers without seeing their personal numbers. Unlike social media, it's algorithm-free — every subscriber sees every post. Build your WhatsApp Channel now — it's a future-proof audience.

**Q7: How do I attract branded content partnerships?**
Create a professional media kit showing your monthly reach, audience demographics (city, age, income), and past branded content success cases. Pitch directly to marketing managers and PR agencies of brands aligned with your audience. Price fairly and deliver quality — the best branded content clients become annual partners.
