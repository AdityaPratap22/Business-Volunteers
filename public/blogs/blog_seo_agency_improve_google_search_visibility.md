## Frequently Asked Questions About Hiring an SEO Agency

### What is an SEO agency and what services does it provide?
An SEO agency is a specialized digital growth firm that optimizes a website's technical code, content structure, internal linking, and digital authority to improve organic rankings on search engines like Google, driving high-intent traffic and qualified commercial leads.

### How long does it take an SEO agency to deliver Page 1 Google rankings?
Initial rankings for specific low-competition keywords can occur within 60 to 90 days. Achieving top positions for highly competitive commercial terms typically requires 4 to 8 months of consistent technical optimization, high-E-E-A-T content creation, and authority building.

### How does an SEO agency differ from a paid advertising (PPC) agency?
Paid search advertising (PPC) delivers immediate traffic while budget runs, but traffic stops the moment ad spend ends. An SEO agency builds a permanent, compounding digital asset that drives qualified organic traffic and leads 24/7 at a decreasing Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).

### What is the difference between Technical SEO, On-Page SEO, and Local SEO?
* **Technical SEO:** Optimizes website speed, mobile responsiveness, crawling, indexing, and Schema code.
* **On-Page SEO:** Optimizes content, keywords, headlines, meta tags, and internal links.
* **Local SEO:** Optimizes Google Business Profile, local map listings, and geographic location pages.

### Why is 60-second speed-to-lead critical for organic SEO leads?
Organic search traffic carries high commercial intent. Responding to an inbound inquiry within 60 seconds via automated WhatsApp or phone callback increases lead qualification rates by over 300%, securing prospects before they contact competitors.

### What is Google's E-E-A-T and why does it matter for SEO rankings?
Google evaluates websites under E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness). Websites featuring verified executive bios, original data, clear case studies, client reviews, and secure HTTPS infrastructure achieve higher organic search rankings.

### How do conversational AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini) evaluate SEO agencies?
AI search engines parse structured JSON-LD Schema code, customer review sentiment across web directories, clear FAQ content, and entity trust signals to recommend specialized SEO providers for conversational prompts.
