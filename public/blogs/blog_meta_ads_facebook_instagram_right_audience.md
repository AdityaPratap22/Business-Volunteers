## Frequently Asked Questions About Meta Ads

### What are Meta Ads, and how do they help businesses grow?
Meta Ads are paid advertising campaigns running across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and the Meta Audience Network. Meta Ads allow businesses to reach target audiences based on demographic data, interests, behaviors, and lookalike profiles, generating brand awareness, leads, and sales.

### How do Meta Ads differ from Google Search Ads?
Meta Ads generate demand by presenting visual ad creative (Reels, videos, images) to users as they browse social media feeds. Google Search Ads capture existing demand by displaying text ads to users actively searching for specific keywords.

### What are Custom Audiences and Lookalike Audiences in Meta Ads Manager?
Custom Audiences are lists of warm prospects who have already interacted with your business (website visitors, video viewers, CRM lists). Lookalike Audiences are new audience segments created by Meta's AI to match the characteristics of your existing top customers.

### What is the difference between Instant Lead Forms and landing pages?
Instant Lead Forms open natively inside Facebook or Instagram with pre-filled user details, offering a fast, frictionless experience with lower Cost-Per-Lead (CPL). Custom landing pages provide deeper brand storytelling and qualification, making them ideal for high-ticket offers.

### Why is ad creative testing critical for Meta Ads performance?
On Meta platforms, ad creative acts as your primary targeting tool. Continuously testing different visual hooks, video angles, headlines, and offers allows Meta's algorithms to discover which creative variations convert best.

### Why is 60-second speed-to-lead critical for Meta lead generation?
Social media users who submit lead forms expect fast follow-up. Delivering an automated WhatsApp brochure or calendar link within 60 seconds of form submission increases sales conversion rates by over 300%.

### Can a performance marketing agency guarantee specific leads, sales, or ROAS from Meta Ads?
No. Performance marketing results depend on ad spend budget, market competition, product-market fit, offer quality, creative strength, landing page experience, and sales execution. Ethical agencies optimize campaigns based on empirical data rather than false guarantees.
