## 4. Responsible B2B Marketing & Truthful Technical Communication

Electronics manufacturing marketing must prioritize factual accuracy, verified compliance, and transparent specifications. Manufacturers must focus marketing communications on production capabilities, component specifications, and brand reliability—**never on exaggerated claims or fabricated certifications**.

```
Ethical Industrial Marketing vs Misleading Claims:
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Misleading & Unverified Claims    | Ethical & Transparent B2B Marketing              |
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
|  Fabricating ISO / RoHS compliance | Displaying verified, authentic certification proofs|
|  Exaggerating factory capacity & SLAs| Presenting accurate production capabilities & SLAs|
|  Using stock photos of fake labs   | Showcasing real factory machinery & team photos   |
|  Promising unverified lead times   | Outlining honest manufacturing & shipping schedules|
+------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4 Rules of Ethical Electronics Marketing
1. **Never Fabricate Compliance Badges:** Display ISO, RoHS, CE, or regional quality accreditations only when verified certificates are actively held.
2. **Present Accurate Technical Specs:** Ensure voltage ratings, operating temperatures, and performance parameters match actual component testing datasheets.
3. **Showcase Real Facility Renders & Videos:** Feature actual factory floors, automated assembly lines, and testing equipment rather than misleading stock imagery.
4. **Protect Client Confidentiality (NDAs):** Respect non-disclosure agreements by anonymizing proprietary OEM designs unless explicit written consent is granted.
