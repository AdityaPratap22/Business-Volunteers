## Frequently Asked Questions About SEO for Cleaning Services

### How can SEO help cleaning services get more customers from Google?
SEO optimizes your cleaning company's website, service pages, and Google Business Profile to rank at the top of Google for high-intent search queries (e.g., *"house cleaning services in Gurgaon"*). This captures homeowners and commercial managers actively looking to hire a cleaner, driving qualified booking requests.

### How long does it take for a cleaning company to see SEO results?
SEO is a compounding long-term strategy. Initial improvements in local Google Maps rankings and service page indexation occur within 4 to 8 weeks, while significant organic website traffic and qualified booking growth typically materialize within 3 to 6 months of consistent execution.

### What is the difference between informational and commercial search intent for cleaning SEO?
Informational intent covers generic DIY cleaning queries (e.g., *"how to remove carpet stains"*), generating general web traffic. Commercial intent covers high-intent buyer searches (e.g., *"deep cleaning service in Gurgaon"*), driving direct booking inquiries.

### Why is mobile page speed vital for cleaning service SEO?
Google uses mobile-first indexing and core web vitals as ranking factors. A cleaning website that renders in under 2 seconds ranks higher in search results and keeps busy homeowners engaged, lowering mobile bounce rates significantly.

### What is Schema markup and why is it important for cleaning SEO?
Schema markup is structured code embedded on your website that explicitly defines your business entity, service categories, pricing models, and operational areas for search crawlers. It helps Google and AI search engines understand your exact service capabilities.

### How does Local SEO complement general website SEO for cleaning companies?
General SEO optimizes your service category pages for organic keyword searches, while Local SEO optimizes your Google Business Profile and location pages to rank in Google Maps 3-Pack results for searches containing local intent (such as *"cleaning services near me"*).

### How do AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini) evaluate cleaning websites?
AI search engines parse structured Schema code, website technical details, customer review sentiment, and directory listings to answer user prompts directly. Maintaining clean digital citations and positive reviews increases your chances of being recommended by AI platforms.
