## 3. Mobile Strategy Intake Funnels & Speed-to-Lead

Securing wedding catering packages or corporate retainers should not be managed via casual direct messages or informal notes. Establishing a structured digital intake funnel elevates your professional standing and filters out uncommitted inquiries.

```
Structured Catering Intake Funnel Architecture:
[ Educational Social Post / Video ] ➔ [ Mobile Event Landing Page ] ➔ [ 3-Field Form ] ➔ [ 60-Sec WhatsApp Confirmation ]
```

### Designing a Mobile Event & Wedding Intake Form
Direct interested clients to a mobile-optimized landing page requesting key details:
* **Event Type & Guest Count:** What specific event (e.g., *Wedding Banquet, Corporate Summit, Private Party*) are they planning?
* **Date & Venue Location:** What is their target date and event location?
* **Contact Details:** Mobile / Business WhatsApp number for delivering customized sample menus and confirming quote details.

### Automating Immediate Contact
Connect your intake forms to an automated CRM system. Delivering an automated WhatsApp proposal within 60 seconds of inquiry demonstrates high operational responsiveness, increasing contract closing rates significantly.
