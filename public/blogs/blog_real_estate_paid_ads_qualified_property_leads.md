## Frequently Asked Questions About Real Estate Paid Ads

### What are real estate paid ads, and how do they work?
Real estate paid ads are online performance campaigns running across Google Search, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn that target prospective property buyers, investors, and commercial tenants with visual property walkthroughs, floor plans, and site visit offers.

### Which digital platform works best for real estate lead generation?
Google Search Ads work best for capturing active, high-intent property searchers looking for specific configurations in targeted cities. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) work best for generating visual excitement, brand awareness, and high-volume inquiries for new project launches.

### What is the difference between Meta Instant Forms and dedicated landing pages?
Meta Instant Forms open natively within Facebook or Instagram with pre-filled contact details, providing a fast, low-friction experience with higher lead volume. Dedicated mobile landing pages provide immersive property visuals, floor plans, and deeper lead qualification.

### Why is 60-second speed-to-lead critical for real estate inquiries?
Property searchers evaluate multiple projects simultaneously. Delivering an automated WhatsApp digital brochure or site visit booking link within 60 seconds increases confirmed site visit rates by over 300%.

### How does retargeting help real estate developers close sales?
Real estate buying decisions take weeks or months. Retargeting ads display ongoing construction progress videos, buyer testimonials, and inventory updates to warm prospects across social media and YouTube, keeping your project top-of-mind.

### What ethical guidelines apply to real estate advertising?
Real estate ads must display accurate property pricing, true location connectivity, authentic renders, and verified regulatory approval details (such as RERA registration numbers). Marketers must never guarantee property appreciation or financial returns.

### Can an agency guarantee specific property sales, site visits, or ROAS?
No. Real estate sales depend on macro-economic conditions, property location, project pricing, construction quality, developer reputation, and sales team execution. Ethical agencies optimize campaigns based on empirical data rather than making false guarantees.
