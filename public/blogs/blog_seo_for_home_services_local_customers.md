## Frequently Asked Questions About SEO for Home Services

### What is SEO for Home Services?
SEO for Home Services is the specialized process of optimizing a contractor's website architecture, individual trade service pages, service-area landing pages, Google Business Profiles, NAP consistency, customer reviews, and Schema markup to rank at the top of Google search results and Google Maps for local queries.

### How does Home Service SEO differ from traditional National SEO?
National SEO targets nationwide keywords without geographic intent. Home Service SEO optimizes for localized commercial queries (e.g., *"electrician in Gurgaon Sector 56"*) by combining domain authority with hyper-local service-area pages and Google Maps 3-Pack listings.

### Why is Google Business Profile optimization critical for contractor SEO?
Google Business Profile powers Google Maps 3-Pack listings, which display above organic search results on mobile devices. Optimizing your GBP listing drives direct phone calls, driving directions, quote requests, and immediate service dispatches.

### Should individual trade services have dedicated pages on a contractor website?
Yes. Creating individual, keyword-optimized pages for Plumbing Repair, Drain Cleaning, Electrical Wiring, AC Servicing, and Painting allows your website to rank for specific service queries while providing clear information to prospective clients.

### Why is 60-second speed-to-lead important for home service SEO traffic?
Delivering an automated WhatsApp response within 60 seconds of inquiry increases lead qualification rates by over 300%, securing local clients before they call competing contractors.

### How do conversational AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini) evaluate contractors?
AI search engines parse structured JSON-LD Schema code, Google Maps location data, customer review sentiment across web platforms, and clear FAQ content to recommend verified local contractors for conversational prompts.
